# RSU Vesting Schedule

Copy to `$MANGO_HOME/rsu-schedule.md` (default `~/.mango/rsu-schedule.md`).
Only needed for the RSU tools; skip it entirely otherwise.

Each grant is a `## <year> Grant: ...` heading followed by a `### Vesting
Schedule` table. Vests dated in the past are excluded from "upcoming" totals.
